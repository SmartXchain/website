For about ninety minutes yesterday morning, three lending protocols on Solana saw simultaneous, correlated redemptions that hit a combined notional of $1.8B. By any reasonable definition that's a bank run. By the definition the protocols themselves wrote into their own docs, it should have triggered at least one cascade liquidation.

It didn't. And the reason it didn't is worth understanding — because it's not the reason the marketing teams are giving.

## The setup

A leveraged farming strategy that had quietly accumulated $400M of exposure across Kamino, MarginFi, and a third protocol I won't name (it doesn't matter; the lesson is structural) started unwinding at 06:18 UTC. The unwind triggered automated redemptions from a half-dozen vaults that were tracking it. Those redemptions cascaded into related vaults. Within twenty minutes, the queue was deep enough that any sensible risk model would have started liquidating.

## What actually saved them

Three things, in order of importance:

1. **Jito's MEV market did its job.** Validators preferred sequencing the unwind cleanly over front-running it, because the bundle tips were better. Coordination through self-interest worked.
2. **The new priority fee market kept transactions deterministic.** Pre-2025, you'd have seen failed transactions stack up and amplify the panic. Yesterday, every redemption confirmed within two slots.
3. **The protocols had — quietly, since the December incident — added circuit breakers on redemption velocity.** Nobody marketed these because nobody wanted to admit they were needed.

## What this proves

Solana, at the chain level, can absorb a $1.8B coordinated redemption without throwing a fit. That's a non-trivial claim and it's now backed by evidence. The protocols on top of Solana — that's still rough. The circuit-breaker silence is telling: the people building DeFi infrastructure are learning from incidents, but they're not yet willing to be transparent about how.

The next stress test will be larger. We should be looking at what we don't yet have circuit breakers for.
