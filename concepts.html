Three directions for a tech news brand built on your domain.

"SmartXChain" reads naturally as smart × exchange × chain — which opens the door to three very different brands. Today's Big Idea

The agent economy needs a unit of account that isn't the dollar

Every "agent commerce" demo so far treats the agent like a buyer with a credit card. That doesn't scale — buyers settle in seconds, agents need to settle in milliseconds, and credit card rails can't do either.

The interesting bet right now: stablecoin rails + intent-based execution. If you want to know why Stripe just acquired Bridge for $1.1B, that's why. The next 18 months get fun.

My recommendation

Start with Brief. Earn the right to become Daily. Spin out Frontier as a vertical.

A daily newsletter (Concept C) is the cheapest and fastest way to build a real audience and prove your voice — and the domain works perfectly as a brand name. Once you have 5–10K engaged subscribers, layer in long-form web content (Concept B's strengths). If a particular vertical (AI agents, Web3 infra, deep tech) keeps outperforming, spin it out as a paid "Frontier" tier — that's where the domain name really pays off.

  2. Decide editorial angle: AI-heavy, Web3-heavy, balanced, or other
